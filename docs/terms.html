What Haven Hub is

Haven Hub is free, open-source software for teams organizing a Hack Club Haven event, published under the MIT licence. It is made by Haven organizers and is not an official Hack Club HQ product.

Who is responsible for a hub

Each hub is set up and run by its event's organizers. They choose who is on it, what it shows publicly and what they do with its data, and they follow Hack Club's rules for Haven events and the Hack Club Code of Conduct. How a hub handles data is described on the privacy page.

Using it fairly

  • Use a hub only for organizing your event and only with people who agreed to be on your team.
  • Keep personal links, passwords and reset links to yourself. If one leaks, ask an admin to reset it.
  • Don't try to get into hubs or data that aren't yours, overload the service, or use it to send spam.
  • Only list sponsors and partners who agreed to it, and only upload pictures you may share.

No warranty

Haven Hub is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, as the MIT licence says. Keep your own backups of anything important — a hub can export all its data at any time.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, the date at the top changes too. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
